A Q&A blog built from running a dental clinic and marketing agency while managing ADHD off-grid in Nuweiba, Egypt.

Every post answers a real question. Every answer includes a confidence indicator showing how validated the thinking is.

## Confidence Levels

**██░░░░░░░░ 20% - I'm Winging This**  
Shower thought. Barely tested. Probably wrong.

**████░░░░░░ 40% - I Think This Is True**  
Early patterns. Limited sample size.

**██████░░░░ 60% - I've Tested This**  
Battle-tested. Know the failure modes.

**████████░░ 80% - I've Paid for This Opinion**  
Learned through expensive mistakes.

**██████████ 100% - Matter of Fact**  
Core principle. Non-negotiable.

Most blogs hide uncertainty. I show it. You know what's validated and what's exploratory.

## The Method

1. **Raw thinking first** - Messy, unfiltered thoughts
2. **AI as structural editor** - Claude organizes, I provide insight
3. **Real examples only** - Grounded in actual operations
4. **Honest confidence levels** - Transparent about validation

## The Voice

Sarcastic. Direct. Sometimes self-roasting. Not motivational. Not corporate.

**Pattern:** Concept → Real example that makes it stick.

Example: I "knew" crowns were most profitable until numbers showed scaling printed more money per hour.

## Who This Is For

**You'll find value if:**
- Small business owner (under 20 people)
- Tired of advice for unlimited resources
- Prefer honesty over polish

**Not for you if:**
- Want step-by-step tutorials
- Need motivational content
- Prefer conventional wisdom
