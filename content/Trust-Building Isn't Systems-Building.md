I used to think my partner's hour-long phone calls were a waste of time. I could send the same information in three messages. But I was measuring the wrong thing. He wasn't sending information. He was building a relationship strong enough to survive the day we disappoint someone.

I can't do that. I can build a process that prevents disappointment. I can't build the trust that absorbs it when the process fails, and every process fails eventually. A system that never plans for its own failure isn't finished. It's just untested.

Trust-building isn't a slower version of systems-building. It's a different skill. It doesn't show up in a diagram, because a diagram has no place for it. Treating it as the weaker skill is how a systems person ends up undervaluing exactly what saves the relationship when the system breaks.
