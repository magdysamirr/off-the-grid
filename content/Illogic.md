Illogical reasoning is reasoning that is not reasonable or does not follow a carefully considered plan or system. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/illogical)

For a management decision, test whether the conclusion follows from the stated evidence and premises, whether it serves the stated objective, and whether the same reasoning would be accepted in a comparable case.

An unexpected decision is not automatically illogical. The person may have information or constraints that are not yet visible. Surface the gap in reasoning and ask what premise is missing before judging motive or competence.
