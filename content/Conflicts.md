A conflict is an active disagreement between people with opposing opinions or principles, or a situation where opposing demands or ideas force a choice. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/conflict)

For management, distinguish conflict from contradiction. A contradiction is about claims that cannot both be true. A conflict is about interests, needs, values, goals, or expectations that pull in different directions. A conflict can exist even when everyone agrees on the facts.

Name what is in tension, who is affected, what each side needs, and which authority or agreement governs the decision. Do not reduce a structural conflict to a personality problem before checking incentives, roles, and constraints.
