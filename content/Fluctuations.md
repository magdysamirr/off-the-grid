A fluctuation is a change, especially movement between levels or values. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/fluctuation)

In an operating system, a fluctuation is an observed change in a measure or behaviour. It is a signal to compare periods and look for causes, not automatically evidence of a trend, failure, or opportunity.

Check that the measure is comparable across periods, the source is current, and the change is larger or more persistent than ordinary variation. Then ask what changed in the work, context, or measurement itself. A flat line can matter too when the objective requires progress.
