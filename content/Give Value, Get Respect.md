Respect is not a position you occupy. It is a return on value you give.

A senior title, more experience, a higher place in the hierarchy. None of it earns respect by itself. What earns it is what you hand down: context, teaching, time, the benefit of the doubt. Give those, and your team gives back respect. Give none, and you are just a person with authority waiting for an apology that never comes.

## The exchange

Your team will tolerate a lot from someone who gives them real value. They will tolerate your inconsistency, your distance, your bad days, your off-grid gaps in availability. They tolerate it because the value you give makes the friction worth it. Tolerance is the rent they pay for working with you. You only collect it when the value is real.

The manager who holds the map, shares nothing, and waits for mistakes collects nothing. They get compliance, not loyalty. People do what they are told because they are afraid, not because they are invested. Fear does not compound. Value does.

## What giving value looks like

It is not grand gestures. It is the small steady exchange:

- Teaching the thing you know before the mistake can happen
- Sharing context the other person does not have
- Taking the blame that is yours and not deflecting it
- Giving the benefit of the doubt when the evidence is unclear
- Fixing the process, not just the person

Each of these is a deposit. Over time, deposits compound into respect, and respect compounds into love, and tolerance for your own [[Running a Business with ADHD|inconsistency]]. You cannot demand that account. You can only make deposits and wait.

## What this prevents

The senior who does not teach is the same senior who wonders why nobody respects them. They are not puzzled about the cause. They are puzzled that value was required at all. They thought the title was the currency. It never is.

Respect is a return, not a right. [[Toxic Blame Culture|Blame]] is what you get when you skip the giving and reach for the punishment. Teaching is what you give instead. The choice is yours, and the team keeps the books.
