A contradiction is a logical incompatibility between propositions: both cannot be true in the same respect at the same time. [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/contradiction/)

In management records, two claims that appear opposed are a contradiction candidate. First check whether they concern the same subject, time period, definition, and scope. “The clinic is open” and “the clinic is closed” may conflict if they describe the same hours today; they may both be true if one describes the reception desk and the other the treatment rooms.

Keep both source claims, dates, and authority visible until resolved. Do not silently choose the newest statement. The mismatch may reveal a stale record, changed circumstances, ambiguous wording, or a genuine error.
