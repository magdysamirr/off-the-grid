Something is irrelevant when it is not related to what is being considered and therefore does not matter to that question or decision. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/irrelevant)

In management, irrelevance is contextual. A true fact can still be irrelevant to the decision at hand. The test is not whether a detail is interesting, but whether it changes the objective, options, constraints, [[Risk|risk]], or next action.

Use this filter to protect attention, not to dismiss people or inconvenient evidence. If uncertain, ask what decision the information could reasonably affect before removing it from view.
