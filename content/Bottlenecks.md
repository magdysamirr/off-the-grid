A bottleneck is the narrowest point in a process: the one step, person or resource that sets the pace for everything behind it.

In an operating system, the whole moves only as fast as its slowest necessary step. Work piles up in front of it and sits idle after it. Speeding up any other part changes nothing.

Ask: **Where does work wait? Who or what does everything queue behind?** Look for approvals that only one person can give, a shared tool or room everyone needs at once, a step that is always "almost done", and a backlog that grows while the rest of the team is free.

A bottleneck is not a [[Weak Links|weak link]]. A weak link breaks the system when it fails. A bottleneck limits it while working perfectly. The same person can be both.

Fix the constraint first: protect its time, move work off it, or add capacity there. Effort anywhere else is waste until the bottleneck moves.
