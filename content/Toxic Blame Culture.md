Blame culture is what you get when teaching is replaced by verdicts. [[Holding the Map, Hiding the Map|The full argument]] is that whoever holds more context carries the obligation to close the gap before punishing anyone who falls into it. This is the test that follows from it.

## The test

Before you call something accountability, ask: did they know the rule before they broke it? Was the expectation stated in advance? Was the teaching offered while there was still time to use it?

If the answer is no to any of these, you are not enforcing standards. You are [[Holding the Map, Hiding the Map|setting a trap]]. And the person falling into it is the one you failed, not the one who failed you.

The fix is not softer management. It is honest management: teach first, then hold to the standard you actually taught.
