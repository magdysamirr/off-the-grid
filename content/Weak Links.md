A weak link is a weak part, especially the weakest part, of a larger whole. [Cambridge Dictionary](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/weak-link)

In an operating system, the weak link is a person, tool, handoff, or step whose failure can interrupt or undermine the outcome. The concern is not that the component is imperfect. It is that the whole process depends on it and has no workable fallback.

Ask: **What does this process depend on, and what happens if that dependency is unavailable, late, wrong, or forgotten?** Look for single points of failure, knowledge held by one person, manual steps with no receipt, and handoffs where responsibility disappears.

A weak link is a vulnerability to examine, not a verdict about a person's worth. Prefer strengthening the process, adding a proportionate fallback, or reducing unnecessary dependence over adding layers of control.
