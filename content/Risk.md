ISO 31000 defines risk as the **effect of uncertainty on objectives**. This includes uncertainty that could produce harmful consequences or beneficial opportunities. [Victorian Managed Insurance Authority, citing AS ISO 31000:2018](https://www.vmia.vic.gov.au/what-risk)

To make a risk useful for management, name the objective, the uncertain event or condition, and the possible effect. Then consider likelihood, consequence, time horizon, and what would reduce exposure or improve readiness. Do not report a risk as a certainty.

Risk is not only danger. It also includes the possibility that uncertainty prevents a valuable outcome. A practical response may be to avoid, reduce, share, accept, or pursue the exposure, depending on the objective and the trade-off.
